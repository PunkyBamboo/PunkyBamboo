### HCI researcher · UX designer · accessibility advocate

I research and design digital experiences with a soft spot for **accessibility and inclusion**. I want products to be comfortably usable by everyone, regardless of their abilities or backgrounds.

I'm currently pursuing a Master's degree in User Experience Design at Technische Hochschule Ingolstadt and working on HCI research at AImotion Bavaria's HCIS Lab.

## What I care about 💝

> Design is only truly beautiful when it opens its doors to everyone, not just a few.

To me, UX is more than creating aesthetic interfaces—although that certainly matters too. It is about shaping meaningful experiences where people feel in tune with a product and everything simply makes sense.

My work sits at the intersection of **inclusive UX, human–computer interaction and playful experiences**. I enjoy turning research questions into thoughtful studies, prototypes and interfaces.
